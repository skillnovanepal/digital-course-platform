NEED TO KNOW MORE?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some important things to know before choosing a plan.

What is Skill Nova Nepal?

Skill Nova Nepal is a digital learning website concept featuring different skill areas and learning plans.

How do I choose a plan?

Compare the listed topics and prices. Contact the website owner to confirm the final package details before paying.

Will I get course access immediately?

Course delivery and access arrangements must be confirmed by the website owner before purchases are accepted.

Can I pay through this website now?

No. This static website does not currently process payments. A secure payment system must be integrated and tested separately.

Are member login and referral commissions active?

No. Secure registration, member dashboards, purchase verification, referral tracking, commission calculations, and withdrawals require a backend system.

How can I contact Skill Nova Nepal?

Use the contact section below. The owner must add a real contact email or connect a working contact service before launch.
